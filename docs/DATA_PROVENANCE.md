# Riverside — Data Provenance

**Repository:** [leosand/Riverside](https://github.com/leosand/Riverside)

---

## Primary data sources

1. **Sentinel-2 imagery**
   - **Provider:** ESA / Copernicus Open Access Hub or mirror (e.g., AWS Open Data, Google Earth Engine).
   - **License:** Generally free for research and operational use; check specific terms for your region and mirror.
   - **Refresh cadence:** Revisit time ~5 days (combined Sentinel-2A/2B); actual availability depends on processing level and cloud cover.
   - **Usage in Riverside:** Raw scenes for AOIs; basis for cloud removal, NDVI, and time-series analysis.

2. **Regulatory thresholds (e.g., CSR)**
   - **Provider:** Relevant environmental or water-management authorities (jurisdiction-dependent).
   - **License:** Typically public regulatory documents; verify attribution and update policy.
   - **Usage in Riverside:** Threshold values against which vegetation or erosion indicators are compared to generate alerts.

---

## Derived data

- **Cloud-reduced composites**
  - Generated by applying cloud-removal models (e.g., DSen2-CR or similar) to raw Sentinel-2 scenes.
  - Considered derived data; retain reference to source scene IDs and processing parameters.

- **NDVI and other indices**
  - Computed from cloud-reduced reflectance bands.
  - Store computation formula, band mapping, and any scaling factors.

- **Risk scores / change indicators**
  - Outputs of predictive models trained or configured for vegetation/erosion risk.
  - Document model version, training data summary, and validation metrics.

- **Alerts**
  - Generated when derived indicators exceed regulatory thresholds.
  - Each alert should reference:
    - AOI identifier
    - Timestamp(s) of source imagery
    - Indicator value and threshold
    - Model/version and processing run ID

---

## Metadata & traceability

For each processing run, record:

- Source scene IDs and acquisition times.
- Processing parameters (cloud-removal model, index formulas, model versions).
- Run timestamp and environment (container image tag, code commit SHA).
- Summary statistics (number of scenes processed, alerts generated, failures).

This metadata supports:

- Reproducibility of results.
- Auditing of alerts for regulatory or scientific purposes.
- Debugging when anomalies are detected.

---

## Limitations & caveats

- **Cloud cover:** Even with cloud removal, persistent cloudiness can degrade data quality.
- **Spatial resolution:** Sentinel-2 (10–20 m) may not capture very fine-scale bank features.
- **Temporal gaps:** Missing or delayed scenes can interrupt time series.
- **Model validity:** Predictive models are site- and period-specific; do not extrapolate beyond validated conditions without re-validation.

---

## Attribution & compliance

- Cite Sentinel-2 / Copernicus appropriately in any public outputs or publications.
- Keep a record of regulatory sources and their versions; update when regulations change.
- Do not present derived indicators or alerts as definitive legal or regulatory determinations; they are decision-support tools subject to human validation.
